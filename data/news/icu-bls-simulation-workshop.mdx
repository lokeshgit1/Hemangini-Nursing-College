To strengthen clinical readiness ahead of hospital placement interviews, the Department of Medical-Surgical Nursing hosted a two-day **Critical Care & Basic Life Support (BLS) Simulation Workshop** in the campus Nursing Skills Lab.

## Practical Stations Covered

Working in small rotating teams, final-year students practiced rapid assessment and team communication across five OSCE-style stations:

1. **Airway Management & High-Quality CPR Protocols** on adult and paediatric trainers.
2. **ECG Lead Placement & Arrhythmia Recognition** for emergency ward monitoring.
3. **IV Infusion Pump Safety & High-Alert Medication Verification** to prevent dosing errors.
4. **Sepsis Bundle & Central Line Care** following strict aseptic technique.
5. **SBAR Handover Communication** for clear escalation to attending physicians.

Hands-on simulation ensures students enter multi-specialty hospital ICUs with steady judgement and safe clinical habits.
